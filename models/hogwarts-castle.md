# Hogwarts Castle 71043 — version 0 reconstruction

## Source and scope

The supplied booklet PDFs are treated as segments of the 71043 instructions; filename digits are not interpreted as printed page or step numbers. Image review of `6607901-50.pdf`, booklet 22, maps PDF pp.6–14 to printed pp.10–18 and steps 1–18. Steps 8–18 appear on printed pp.13–18. Circular side builds shown earlier in this booklet remain a separate unresolved assembly. Minifigures are excluded.

Later in booklet 22, printed pp.50–51 show broad plates and wall supports on the same foundation. Those images remain a follow-up datum for global transforms; they do not yet establish placement of the local step8/14/17/18 modules.

The 64179 frame geometry and recursive dependencies came from the official `ldraw-org/official-library` repository at commit `894bbba5e696b1554800d8e45134c57163dba905`. Its header identifies a Technic Beam 7 x 5 with Open Center 5 x 3, CC BY 4.0. Source, license, dependency paths, and hashes are recorded in `assets/policy.md`. No GoBricks offer or SKU is asserted.

## Written geometry

The root `hogwarts-frame.ldr` contains 25 physical references for steps 1–7. The 64179 frame at `[-40,20,0]` aligns four local through-hole centers `(40,0,z)`, z=−60,−20,20,60, with the four 2780 pins from step1. Center and axis residuals are 0 LDU. The nominal pin spans both adjacent 20-LDU layers; real fit and friction are untested.

Root `part-0006` (32278, step2) currently has an end-face datum against the frame side, but its first hole is 9 LDU beyond the frame side-hole center. The step3 retaining joint remains unverified. `part-0012` and three tan supports form a provisional underside contact at y=38; the overhang and exact step5 support pattern remain unverified. Steps6–7 are candidate placements and remain subject to correction from later attachment evidence.

Printed p.13 step8 is a partial local assembly in `hogwarts-step8.ldr`. It contains a 64179 frame, two opposite-corner 2420 plates, a 32278 rail, two 2780 pins and two 30000 end blocks. The rail rotation sends its local +Y hole axis to +X and its Z hole row along the frame side. The frame mouth centers x=50 and rail hole centers x=60 share axes at z=±60; their 10-LDU axial offset is bridged by the two pins. The 30000 inward pins at z=±140 align with the rail outer-face holes and penetrate about 18.5 LDU into the 20-LDU wall; the brick bodies meet the rail at its outer face. Shape/axis and nominal insertion are checked, physical fit is not. The lime 2x2 axle-hole plate `4032a` and a centered `3700` Technic-brick candidate are now placed as a local core stack: the plate studs meet the brick base at y=−10. The shown side projection is absent from 3700, so this brick ID and its side connector remain tentative; the stack is not claimed attached to the ring.

Printed p.17 steps14–15 begin a local module (`hogwarts-step14.ldr`): a 32524 beam with four pins, a 64179 frame aligned to those pins and a 32278 rail. Printed p.18 step16 adds four 2780 pins; these are placed in the four unoccupied 32278 holes at local z=20,60,100,140, with their +Y axes matching the beam holes and 18/20-LDU nominal insertion. The two called-out 30000 blocks remain unplaced because the page does not establish which support faces/holes they engage. Printed p.18 step17 is now nested in that module as a 64179 frame with four side pins. Its transform maps the pins to x=19,59,99,139, y=30, z=0, with axes −Y; the local 32278 rail holes at those x positions are centered at y=20 with +Y axes. All four axes align, the reference centers differ by 10 LDU along the axis, and the pin spans cross both 20-LDU layers. The combined step14–17 assembly remains root-unplaced because its global connection to steps1–13 has not been established.

Printed p.14 step9 is now represented in `hogwarts-step9.ldr`: the brown bar is official `2465` (Brick 1 x 16) and the gray plate is official `4282` (Plate 2 x 16). Their perpendicular perimeter orientation is visible in the result image. The local corner arrangement is tentative, because the diagram does not expose a precise stud datum or global transformation onto the step1–7 foundation. Neither piece is root-reachable yet. Printed p.15 step10 now has a partial local `hogwarts-step10.ldr` with the tan left-tapered 67142 and gray right-tapered 68196 Technic Panels 5x13. The mirrored shapes and hole profiles match the callout; their 236-LDU seam and lower support contact remain tentative, so the pair is not root-reachable. The remaining step10 callout parts and steps11–13 still need shape-specific placement. Their callouts/sequence show additional beams and wall/base details, plus four step16 pins and two side-pin blocks. Step18 now has a tentative local `hogwarts-step18.ldr` with candidate 32140 and two 2780 pins. The candidate DAT has +Y holes at the two selected pin centers, but the callout has no element number; part identity and exact selected holes remain unconfirmed. This module is not root reachable. Model state keeps local definitions separate from root placed-piece counts.
