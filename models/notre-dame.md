# Notre-Dame de Paris v0

## Reconstruction status

The v0 model is in progress in [`notre-dame-v0.mpd`](notre-dame-v0.mpd). Booklets 6634573-66 (steps1–88), 6634573-125 (steps89–160), 6634573-163 (steps161–192), 6634573-202 (steps193–249), and 6634573-239 (steps250–298) are represented as a mix of dimensioned anchors and diagram-derived placements. Later assembly views have been used to revise the approach, paired entry and nave roof runs. Booklet 6634573-290, steps299–393, is now represented; architectural and ornamental candidates are documented with the remaining shape and clutch uncertainties.

Current project policy excludes ordinary minifigures, signs and nameplates. This does not exclude architectural sculpture or ornament. The manual depicts 12 apostolic statuettes in steps377 and385. The manual inventory lists element 6335936 ×12 in Sand Green. Rebrickable’s public element crosswalk maps it to part 90398/design 53017; this is a community crosswalk, not an official LEGO declaration. Official LDraw 90398 “Minifig Statuette” is therefore a supported shape candidate, while exact mold identity and physical fit remain provisional. GoBricks product ID 90398 is an unrelated Technic connector; no GoBricks match is recorded.

This v0 reconstruction may register and use original LDraw parts without GoBricks mapping, as allowed by the current policy. GoBricks correspondence and sales status remain unverified; no product IDs or SKUs have been inferred. `tools/validate_model.py` checks GoBricks/catalog metadata and is not the v0 geometry acceptance test. LDraw references, counts, coordinates, rotations and selected stud grids are checked separately. Physical clutch/fit remains unverified.
